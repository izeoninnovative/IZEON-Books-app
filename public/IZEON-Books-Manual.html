1. System Overview & Core Capabilities

IZEON Books is the centralized financial operating system engineered specifically for IZEON INNOVATIVE Academy of Computer Software Training. The application bridges the gap between student admissions, monthly fee collection, automated GST invoicing, operating overheads, staff salary disbursement, and high-level Profit & Loss auditing.

Key System Highlights

Automated EMI schedule creation, strict excess-payment blocking, auto-clearance of pending installments upon full fee settlement, and real-time executive financial snapshots.

3. Student Admission & Fee Structuring

Enrolling a student dynamically builds their complete financial schedule:

  • Demographics: Candidate full name, verified email, WhatsApp/Phone contact, Date of Birth, and residential address.
  • Course Selection: Automatically determines base tuition fee and course duration.
  • Payment Plan:
    • Lumpsum: Single payment covering tuition in full with optional promotional discount.
    • EMI Installment Plan: Flexible down payment upfront; the remaining balance is divided into 2 to 12 monthly installments with customizable due dates (e.g. 5th or 10th of every month).
  • Tax Classification: Supports GST Tax Invoice (18% split into 9% CGST + 9% SGST) or standard academy receipt format.

4. Financial Rules & Smart EMI Payment Engine

1. Automatic Installment Clearance (Auto-Clear Engine)

When a student decides to clear their entire remaining balance at once, the system records the payment and automatically marks all subsequent pending EMI installments (#5, #6, #7, etc.) as PAID / Cleared in Full. No manual status editing is required.

2. Strict Excess Payment Prevention (Zero Overpayment)

The financial engine strictly protects against accepting any amount that exceeds the student's actual outstanding balance. Both the client-side modal and backend APIs reject excess collections, ensuring books always balance with exact revenue figures.

One-Click Quick Action Options

Inside the EMI payment modal, the operator is provided with smart shortcuts:

  • [ Pay Due EMI ] — Automatically loads the exact installment amount due.
  • [ Pay Full Balance ] — Automatically loads the total outstanding balance for full fee closure in a single click.

5. Invoicing & Official GST Compliance

Every payment captured generates an immutable invoice record with sequential format INV-YYYYMMDD-XXXX:

  • Legal Entity Header: IZEON INNOVATIVE, Academy of Computer Software Training.
  • GSTIN Display: Official 15-digit GST identification number printed prominently on all tax invoices.
  • Tax Segregation: Clear calculation of Base Taxable Value, CGST (9%), SGST (9%), and Net Payment Amount.
  • Printer Compatibility: Optimized for standard A4 desktop printing and PDF archival.

6. Profit & Loss Auditing & Expense Tracker

Executive management can review profitability across any selected month or entire fiscal year using the dynamic P&L formula:

Net Operating Surplus Equation

Net Profit = [ Total Student Collections ] - [ Operational Expenses + Staff Salaries ]

  • Operating Overheads: Categorized tracking for Academy Rent, Electricity & Utilities, Marketing & Ads, Hardware/Equipment, and Office Maintenance.
  • Trainer & Staff Payroll: Disbursement records detailing Employee Name, Designation, Month/Year, Paid Amount, and Bank Transfer / UTR reference.

7. Notification & Feedback System

Native browser alerts have been replaced with a high-performance floating Toast notification system matching the academy's branding:

  • Success: Emerald alert with auto-dismiss countdown on invoice creation, payment recording, and admissions.
  • Error: Crimson alert when invalid credentials or excess payment attempts occur.
  • Warning / Info: Amber & Sapphire blue notices for session changes and informational system hints.
